# Privacy Policy for Eazzio School

**Effective Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 23, 2026  

Eazzio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Eazzio School** mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you access or use Eazzio School as a student, parent, teacher, or school administrator, we process information necessary to provide educational and institutional services:
- **Account & Identity Information:** Name, student ID, employee ID, school-assigned email address, and role.
- **Academic & Operational Data:** Classroom attendance records, examination marks, grade sheets, homework assignments, routine schedules, and school circulars.
- **Financial Information (Fees):** Tuition fee invoices, transaction reference identifiers, payment timestamps, and payment statuses processed securely via verified payment gateway partners (such as Razorpay). We do not store full credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers.
- **Device & Technical Information:** Network state, device model, operating system version, and application performance metrics for troubleshooting and push notification delivery.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Authenticate authorized students, faculty members, and administrators.
- Deliver timetable routines, academic results, and attendance records.
- Facilitate the secure review and payment of institutional fees and provide digital receipts.
- Dispatch school notices, emergency alerts, and assignment deadlines via notifications.
- Ensure app performance, diagnostic stability, and prevent unauthorized account access.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure
We respect your privacy and **do not sell, rent, or trade** personal student or faculty data to third parties or advertisers. Information is only shared under the following conditions:
- **With Your Educational Institution:** Your data is managed under the direct authority of the school or educational institution with which you are enrolled or employed.
- **Payment Processors:** Transaction details are processed via encrypted channels by RBI-authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay) solely to execute tuition fee settlements.
- **Legal Compliance:** When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation.

---

### 4. Data Security & Storage
- All network communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
- Authentication tokens (JWT) are stored locally on the device using hardware-backed encrypted storage mechanisms (Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences).
- Access to institutional databases is restricted to authenticated users with role-specific authorization.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
- Student and academic records are retained for the duration determined by your school's data retention policies and applicable educational regulations.
- Users wishing to request data deletion or account removal may contact their school administration or reach out directly to our privacy officer at the contact details below.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Eazzio School is designed for educational use by schools, students, and their parents. Any collection of personal data belonging to minors is conducted strictly on behalf of and under the contractual direction of the participating school or educational institution in compliance with applicable child privacy standards.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the service following such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
